To My Special Person ❤️

Nuvvu naa life lo enter ayyaka, konni things automatically beautiful ga maaripoyayi. Mundu normal ga anipinchina chala small moments kuda, ippudu nee tho share chesukunnappudu chaala special ga anipisthayi.

Honestly, ninnu ela describe cheyyalo sometimes naake words dorakavu. Because nuvvu naaku just oka person kaadhu… you have become a very special part of my life.

Nuvvu naatho matladina prathi sari, nee voice vinagane, nee messages chudagane… naaku teliyakundane face lo smile vachesthundi. Konni rojulu entha hectic ga unna, entha tension unna, just nee phone lo wallpaper chudagane naa mind automatic ga calm aipothundi. ❤️

Nuvvu nannu “Bava” ani pilichinappudu naaku teliyani happiness okati vastundi. Adi just oka word ayina, nee noti nunchi vinipinchinappudu matram chaala special ga anipisthundi. Enni tensions unna, aa okka “Bava” chaalu… naa mood motham change aipothundi. 😂

Mana madhya distance undi… and sometimes ninnu chaala miss avutha. Nuvvu pakkana unte cheppalsina chinna chinna things kuda phone lo cheppukovali ante konchem difficult ga anipisthundi. Konni sarlu nee pakkana kurchuni chaala kaburlu cheppali, kalisi navvukovali anipisthundi… kani em chestha, nuvvu akkada, nenu ikkada.

I miss you more than I usually say.

Mana conversations lo entha nonsense unna, random fights unna, teasing unna, silly jokes unna… avi anni naaku chaala precious. Because at the end of the day, they are our moments.

Manam iddaram kalisi create cheskunna memories thakkuve ayina, avi naaku chaala special. And I know manam kalisi inka chaala beautiful memories create cheskovali.

Nuvvu happy ga unte, somehow naaku kuda happiness vastundi. Nuvvu sad ga unte, reason ento telusukoni somehow nee face lo malli smile teesukuraavali anipisthundi.

I am really lucky to have you in my life.

Distance entha unna, days entha busy ga unna, situations ela unna… oka thing matram change avvadu: You will always be very, very special to me. ❤️

And today, I just want you to know that there is someone sitting miles away from you, missing you, thinking about you, smiling at your pictures, waiting for your calls, and feeling ridiculously happy whenever you call me “Bava.” ❤️

So, Deyyam… keep smiling. ❤️

Keep being crazy.

Keep calling me Bava. 😂

And please keep being the same beautiful person I fell in love with.

Happy Birthday, Deyyam. ❤️

And yes…

I hope you know that somewhere in all those wishes, I am wishing for many more moments and memories with you. ❤️

— Your Bava
